[`../frontend/`](../frontend/), and the canonical evaluation reports are under
[`../product/evaluation/reports/`](../product/evaluation/reports/).

  experimental phases (Stage A and before).
- `dashboard/` — an earlier standalone dashboard, superseded by `frontend/`.
